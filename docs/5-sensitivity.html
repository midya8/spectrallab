01 / CHOOSE A FIXED GRAPH PATH

One edge, two degree changes.

W(t) = W(0) + tE adds t to both symmetric entries of the named edge. Coordinates, nearest-neighbor rules and bandwidths are not differentiated. For an initially missing edge, graph-valid changes are one-sided. Every step and every failed fit stays visible.

Local is not global.

The five-vertex path has a point on a rounding boundary: the archived t = 0.001 edit changes one fitted assignment while the projector moves by less than 0.001 in Frobenius norm. Two tested restart partitions have numerically equal inertia within 1e-12, so this assignment change can reflect floating-point tie-breaking, not a uniquely preferred geometric split. Fresh eigensolves may resolve this rounding ambiguity differently. At t = 1 it becomes a five-cycle with a tied retained boundary, so the selected projector and cluster fit are refused. These controls show numerical outcomes, not a universal instability rate or a claim of a unique best partition.

04 / DIFFERENTIATE THE WHOLE OPERATOR
Do not freeze the degrees.
Let d = W1, s = E1, and Nᵢⱼ = Wᵢⱼ / √(dᵢdⱼ). The symmetric normalized Laplacian L = I - N has derivative
L′ᵢⱼ = -Eᵢⱼ / √(dᵢdⱼ) + ½ Nᵢⱼ (sᵢ/dᵢ + sⱼ/dⱼ).
For retained i = 1,2 and excluded j = 3,...,n, the projector derivative sums
P′ = Σᵢ Σⱼ [(uⱼᵀ L′ uᵢ)/(λᵢ - λⱼ)] (uⱼuᵢᵀ + uᵢuⱼᵀ).
Only cross-boundary denominators appear. Repeated eigenvalues wholly inside the retained subspace or wholly outside it do not create an internal division by zero. The two disconnected triangles retain their entire two-dimensional zero eigenspace. The six-cycle splits a repeated eigenvalue across the retained boundary and is refused. No epsilon is added to an eigengap. A boundary gap at most 1e-10 and a zero-degree vertex are numerical refusal policies, not proof that nearby problems are well-conditioned.
NumPy/LAPACK supplies full symmetric eigenvalue decompositions, including finite refits. The derivatives and scalar browser checks are implemented here. There is no autodiff library, learned edge optimizer, cluster-label gradient, sparse solver, robustness certificate or real-world stability claim. A smaller fixed-cut score after changing the graph does not show a better graph.
